Comprehensive Chess Rules, Notation & Live Preview

1. Standard Algebraic Notation (SAN)

SAN is the standard human-readable notation used in tournaments and books. It identifies pieces by single uppercase letters:

  • K = King, Q = Queen, R = Rook, B = Bishop, N = Knight (Pawns have no piece letter).
  • e4: Move pawn to e4.
  • Nf3: Move Knight to f3.
  • exd5: Pawn on e-file captures piece on d5.
  • Nxd4: Knight captures piece on d4.
  • O-O: Kingside Castling / O-O-O: Queenside Castling.
  • e8=Q: Pawn promotion to Queen.

2. Universal Chess Interface Notation (UCI)

UCI is the standard computer coordinate notation. It directly specifies starting square and destination square without piece initials:

  • e2e4: Move piece from e2 to e4.
  • g1f3: Move piece from g1 to f3.
  • e7e8q: Move pawn from e7 to e8 and promote to Queen.
  • e1g1: Kingside castling (King moves from e1 to g1).

Piece Movement Rules

King: Moves 1 square in any direction.
Queen: Moves any number of squares along ranks, files, or diagonals.
Rook: Moves any number of squares horizontally or vertically.
Bishop: Moves any number of squares diagonally.
Knight: Moves in an "L-shape" (2 squares in one direction, 1 square perpendicular); can jump over pieces.
Pawn: Moves 1 square forward (or 2 squares from starting rank); captures 1 square diagonally forward.

Special Rules

Castling: King moves 2 squares towards a Rook, and the Rook hops over to the adjacent square. Allowed if neither piece has moved, all intermediate squares are clear, and the King is not in check or passing through check.
En Passant: If an opponent moves a pawn 2 squares forward landing beside your pawn, you may capture it diagonally as if it had advanced only 1 square.
Pawn Promotion: When a pawn reaches the 8th rank, it must immediately be promoted to a Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.

Endgame & Draw Conditions

Checkmate: The King is attacked and has no legal moves to escape.
Stalemate: The active player is not in check but has zero legal moves left (results in a draw).
50-Move Rule & 3-Fold Repetition: Draw triggered if 50 consecutive moves occur without pawn moves/captures, or if identical board state repeats 3 times.
